Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At 7go.tools, accessible from [https://7go.tools/](https://7go.tools/), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by 7go.tools and how we use it.

Local Data Processing

All calculations performed on our tools, including home prices, interest rates, and loan durations, are processed locally in your browser. We do not store or collect financial inputs on our servers.

Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to 7go.tools and other sites on the internet.

Advertising Partners Privacy Policies

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on 7go.tools. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs.
